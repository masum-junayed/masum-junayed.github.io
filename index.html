About

Trustworthy representation learning for cancer diagnosis and computational pathology.

I work on artificial intelligence, computer vision, and medical image analysis, with a primary focus on cancer diagnosis, prognosis, and computational pathology.

My research includes CNNs, transformers, graph neural networks, vision-language models, whole-slide image analysis, weakly supervised learning, spatial transcriptomics, multimodal biomedical data integration, and LLM-based healthcare applications.
