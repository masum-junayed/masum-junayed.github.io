Research Profile

Trustworthy multimodal representation learning for biomedical AI.

My research specializes in artificial intelligence, multimodal representation learning, computer vision, graph neural networks, and trustworthy biomedical AI.

I develop computational methods for learning from complex biomedical data, with applications in computational pathology, medical imaging, cancer analysis, clinical decision support, and precision medicine. A central goal of my work is to build reliable and interpretable AI systems that connect imaging, molecular, and clinical information for translational health applications.
