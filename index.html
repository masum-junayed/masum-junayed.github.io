About

Researching AI methods for medical imaging and cancer analysis.

My research spans medical image classification, segmentation, survival prediction, weakly supervised learning, multimodal learning, and whole-slide image analysis. I am particularly interested in models that can learn robust, interpretable, and clinically useful representations from large-scale biomedical data.

I have worked on mammography, prostate histopathology, skin imaging, retinal imaging, CT imaging, and related biomedical computer vision problems. My broader goal is to develop reliable machine learning methods that can support real-world clinical decision making.
