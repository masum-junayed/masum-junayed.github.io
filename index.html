Research Profile

Multimodal, trustworthy, and graph-based AI for biomedical discovery.

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science and Engineering specializing in artificial intelligence, multimodal representation learning, computer vision, graph neural networks, and trustworthy biomedical AI.

My research develops deep learning methods for computational pathology, medical image analysis, multimodal biomedical data integration, cancer diagnosis and prognosis, and clinical decision support. I am particularly interested in building reliable and interpretable AI systems that integrate morphological, molecular, spatial, and clinical evidence for translational health applications.

Research Interests

Research areas and methodological focus

My work sits at the intersection of modern machine learning and biomedical computing, with an emphasis on methods that are scalable, clinically meaningful, and reliable under real-world data limitations.

01

Multimodal AI & Representation Learning

Learning unified representations from complementary data sources such as histopathology images, molecular measurements, spatial gene expression, and clinical information through cross-modal alignment, fusion, and joint embedding.

02

Computational Pathology & Medical Image Analysis

Developing AI methods for whole-slide histopathology, mammography, lesion localization, segmentation, classification, cancer grading, and outcome prediction from complex medical images.

03

Graph Neural Networks & Structured Learning

Modeling spatial, morphological, and relational dependencies among tissue regions, image patches, or patient-level observations using graph neural networks, graph propagation, and structured representation learning.

04

Transformers, Attention & Foundation Models

Designing Transformer- and attention-based architectures for large-scale biomedical data, including contextual feature aggregation, cross-attention, semantic guidance, and foundation-model-assisted learning.

05

Trustworthy & Uncertainty-Aware AI

Quantifying model confidence, identifying reliable predictive evidence, and improving robustness when biomedical data are ambiguous, incomplete, noisy, or heterogeneous.

06

Weakly & Semi-Supervised Learning

Reducing dependence on costly expert annotations through learning strategies for localization, segmentation, classification, and whole-slide analysis when detailed labels are limited.

07

Spatial Transcriptomics & Biomedical Data Integration

Connecting tissue morphology with spatially resolved gene-expression signals to study tumor heterogeneity, spatial tumor discovery, and biologically meaningful multimodal prediction.

08

Generative AI & Large Language Models

Exploring generative and language-model approaches for biomedical reasoning, pathology semantics, clinical information extraction, medication safety, and knowledge-guided learning.

09

Longitudinal & Patient-Level Modeling

Learning from prior and current observations using temporal discrepancy modeling, cross-attention, graph-based aggregation, and patient-level representations for clinically relevant change and outcome prediction.

10

AI for Precision Medicine & Clinical Decision Support

Translating predictive models into clinically meaningful tools by integrating heterogeneous patient information for diagnosis, prognosis, risk stratification, and personalized decision support.

Selected Research Projects

Current and selected research directions

2024 — Present

Multimodal Learning of Histopathology and Spatial Gene Expression

Develop multimodal representation learning methods that integrate histopathology imaging with spatial gene-expression information. The work explores cross-modal alignment, feature fusion, and uncertainty-aware learning to combine morphological and molecular signals for spatial tumor discovery and biological prediction.

2024 — 2026

Graph and Transformer Learning for Large-Scale Scientific Imaging

Develop graph- and Transformer-based methods for gigapixel whole-slide images using informative-region selection, spatial graph modeling, and contextual representation learning to preserve relationships among relevant tissue regions while reducing redundancy.

2026 — Present

Trustworthy and Uncertainty-Aware Multimodal Representation Learning

Develop uncertainty-guided multimodal learning methods that identify reliable predictive evidence and improve robustness when individual biomedical data sources are ambiguous, incomplete, or inconsistent.

2023 — 2025

Graph-Based Representation Learning for Patient-Level Prediction

Develop attention-guided graph learning approaches that model tissue regions as related graph elements and aggregate spatial and morphological information into patient-level representations for outcome prediction.

2022 — 2024

Weakly Supervised Learning for Longitudinal Image Analysis

Develop weakly supervised methods for prior-and-current medical images using temporal discrepancy modeling, cross-attention, and multi-scale feature fusion to identify clinically relevant changes with limited region-level annotation.

2022 — Present

Weakly Supervised Computational Pathology

Develop whole-slide image frameworks using informative region selection, graph-based tissue modeling, foundation models, and multimodal semantic representations for tasks such as prostate cancer grading and tissue-level prediction.
